FAQ

The questions brokers ask before signing up

Do I need any marketing experience?

No. The AI asks the questions and writes the campaign. If you can describe a property, you can run a campaign.

Where do the ads run?

Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google, TikTok, YouTube, plus email and SMS sequences. The AI picks the right mix per listing.

How is W1 different from hiring an agency?

It’s 80% cheaper, ships in minutes instead of weeks, and learns from every campaign you run. You stay in control of the budget and creative.

Does it integrate with my CRM?

Yes — HubSpot, Follow Up Boss, Salesforce, kvCORE, Pipedrive and Zapier. Leads land in your CRM the moment they submit.

What about compliance and Fair Housing?

Every piece of generated copy is checked against Fair Housing guidelines and your local disclosure rules before it can be published.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no minimums. Cancel from the dashboard and you’re done.
